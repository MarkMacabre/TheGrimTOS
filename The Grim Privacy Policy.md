# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 17, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how **The Grim** (the “Bot”) collects, uses, and stores information when you interact with it on Discord.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Discord User IDs**  
  Stored to track XP, roles, and activity.
- **XP Data**  
  Numeric values recording how much experience you’ve earned.
- **Level‑Reward Configurations**  
  Mapping of server levels to Discord Role IDs.
- **XP Settings**  
  Per‑server settings for XP per message, XP per voice minute, and system on/off.
- **Activity Timestamps**  
  Last message or voice activity times to enforce inactivity kicks.
- **Voice‑Inactivity Settings**  
  Mute/disconnect delays and days‑until‑server‑kick per server.
- **Age‑Verification Settings**  
  The adult‑role ID and verification log‑channel ID.

**We do NOT collect or store** any message text, voice recordings, IP addresses, or any other personal data.

---

## 2. How We Store Data

- All data is saved in plain JSON files on the Bot’s host server (e.g. `xp_data.json`, `activity.json`, etc.).
- No third‑party services or external databases are used.
- Data is not encrypted; file‑system permissions govern who can access it.

---

## 3. How We Use Data

- To operate core features: XP tracking, role rewards, inactivity moderation, and age verification.
- **Never** sold, shared, or otherwise transmitted outside of your server and this host.

---

## 4. Data Retention

- Data remains indefinitely until you manually delete the JSON files or request removal.
- Inactive members may be kicked from the server after configured days, but their data persists.

---

## 5. Your Choices & Requests

- **Data Deletion:** To request deletion of your personal data (User ID, XP, etc.), contact the Bot owner at **TheGrimRobot@gmail.com**.
- **Log Purging:** Server logs in Discord channels are retained according to your server’s settings.

This policy may be updated to reflect Bot changes. Your continued use of the Bot after changes implies acceptance.
